# rml-website

Static marketing site for Results Matrix Limited. `BRIEF.md` is the
authoritative build specification — phased delivery per its §8, one commit
per phase, stop for client review after each phase.

## Design Context

Read `PRODUCT.md` (strategy) and `DESIGN.md` (visual system) before any UI
work. The short version:

- **Register:** brand (marketing/lead-gen; design IS the product). Platform: web.
- **Audience:** government ministries and international development
  organizations evaluating RML's credibility; primary CTA is booking a free
  discovery session, secondary is a paid consultation.
- **Personality:** credible, precise, quietly confident — premium-firm
  restraint, never startup-flashy.
- **Visual system:** "The Evidence Chamber" — flat hairline-separated
  surfaces, logo-derived palette (Boardroom Blue #0057a8, Caribbean Teal
  #1d7a75, Growth Green restricted to data), tokens in
  `src/styles/global.css`.
- **Hard rule:** never invent client names, credentials, statistics, or
  project claims; proof slots stay empty until clearance.
