This is a **sample post** created during the site build to demonstrate the blog template: the hero panel, the serif reading typography, hero images with enforced alt text, tags, and dates. It is marked `draft: true`, so it appears on staging but never in the production build or the RSS feed.

## What belongs here

A real launch post might introduce the firm's practice areas, explain what readers can expect from the blog, and point to the [services pages](/services/) or [resources](/resources/).
